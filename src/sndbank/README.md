# SndBank — sound source for MelodyBox (in development)

SndBank (`SndBank`, SFX 531) plays the notes MelodyBox sends to it. Put it
in the same patch as MelodyBox; it finds its notes by itself. On its own
it is silent and passes the audio through.

The notes come over the [MIDI bus](../common/midibus/README.md): SndBank
plays MIDI channel 1 on voice 1 and channel 2 on voice 2, from any effect
in the chain that sends on them.

- **Sounds:** SongBox's instruments, two voices (one per MelodyBox
  track), one note at a time each: PB, Tr (bass) and OJ, OR, EP, Mr,
  Kl, Sy, Pk (SongBox's other bass sounds OJ, OR, Sy are the same
  instruments as these, so they are left out). The sound and volume (00 … 95, 50 = normal) of
  each voice are stored in SndBank's own knobs; MelodyBox sets them. A
  new volume plays at once, a new sound from the next note.
- **No knobs to play with.** The knobs hold MelodyBox's saved melody
  (after MelodyBox's own knobs) and the two tracks' sounds and volumes, and are
  locked: a turn springs back. They are named Data1 … Data9 and show
  `DON'T` `TOUCH` `THESE` on each page.
- **Sound list:** SndBank publishes its sounds' names and its channels as
  an [info block](../common/fxinfo/README.md); MelodyBox shows these names
  in SOUND1 / SOUND2.
- **Footswitch off:** silent.

Status: both voices, the sounds and their volumes work on the B1Xon 1.30
(G1on family), with the notes over the MIDI bus. Untested on the MS pedals. The sound engine is
SongBox's, unchanged.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `sndbank.c` | Audio function `Fx_SFX_SndBank`: MIDI in (channels 1, 2), SongBox's engine (two voices), knob lock |
| `build.py` | Builds `SndBank.ZDL` (`--out` required until the first release) |

Shared code: `src/common/midibus/midibus.h` (MIDI in),
`src/common/fxinfo/fxinfo.h` (the sound list),
`src/common/notebus/notebus.h` (the voice settings format),
`src/common/zmenu/zmenu.h` (firmware table), `src/songbox/instruments/`
(the engine) and `src/songbox/songbox_instr.h` (its tables, generated by
SongBox's build).
